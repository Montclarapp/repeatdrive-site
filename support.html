Support

RepeatDrive is intentionally small and self-service. If something is not working, email samuel@montclar.app with your Pipedrive company name and a short description of what happened. Do not email OAuth tokens, passwords or other secrets.

Quick checks

  • The source activity must have a due date.
  • Select exactly one activity before choosing Make recurring.
  • Stopping a series does not delete occurrences already created.
  • RepeatDrive does not automatically reinvite external calendar attendees.
